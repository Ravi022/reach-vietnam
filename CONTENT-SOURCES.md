# Reach Vietnam content and image references

This portfolio uses reference material to describe products Reach Vietnam Company Limited can discuss with buyers. Photographs show the referenced product type; they do not show Reach Vietnam-owned inventory, facilities, or a guaranteed lot. Specifications and availability must be confirmed for each quotation.

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [MHB Energy](https://mhb-energy.com/) | Source photographs and catalogue names for charcoal, firewood, stakes and filtration products. Product-level source links appear on each detail page. |
| [Rashmi 6 Paradigm](https://rashmi6paradigm.com/) | Product-level photographs and original charcoal, pellet, carbon and PKS reference specifications. The PKS visual on the source appeared edited, so it is not used. |
| [Doveco](https://www.doveco.com.vn/products) | Dragon fruit, banana and durian photographs. |
| [Royal Spices](https://royalspicesvn.com/products.html) | Fresh coconut, black pepper, cinnamon and cashew kernel photographs. |
| [Viet Fruit Export](https://vietfruitexport.com/products) and [GoBe Imex](https://gobeimex.com/blog/vietnam-fruit-exporter-summer-fruits/) | Produce range context. |
| Reach Vietnam photo submission | Star anise, palm kernel shell, copra and cassava photographs supplied for the catalogue. |
| [Charcoal Vietnam](https://charcoal-vietnam.com/charcoal-from-vietnam/) | Vietnamese charcoal range and sourcing context. |

Images in `public/images/reference/` are compressed copies of the above reference and client-supplied photographs.

Before commercial launch, Reach Vietnam should confirm that it has permission to republish the third-party photographs and replace them with its own product imagery as it becomes available.
